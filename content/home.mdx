# Blog App

Discover community ideas, explore useful links, and share resources worth reading.

- [Browse blogs](/blogs)
- [Create a blog](/blogs/new)

## What you can do

- **[Browse blogs](/blogs):** Explore community posts, search by title, and open a post to visit its reference link.
- **[Create a blog](/blogs/new):** Share a title, author, and link to a useful resource.
- **[Meet the writers](/users):** Discover community members and browse the blogs they have shared.
- **[Reading list](/me):** Sign in to save blogs and keep track of what you want to read or have finished.
- **[Profile and API token](/me):** View your account information and generate or replace your API token.
- **[Sign in](/login) or [Create an account](/register):** Get started saving blogs and managing your profile.
